3.

Key Insights

What the Data Reveals

The analysis connects RFQ conversion, procurement value, category performance, quotation consistency, and aging to management action.

01

Conversion is the largest gap

Observation

43% of RFQs are Not Awarded: 16 of 37 versus 13 Awarded.

Analysis

Not Awarded is the largest single outcome category.

Impact

A significant share of sourcing activity is not converting.

Recommendation

Create a loss-reason framework by outcome, buyer, category, and supplier.

02

Procurement creates measurable value

Observation

₱82.3M approved value generated ₱10.7M savings at a 13.0% savings rate.

Analysis

Electrical contributes ₱68.5M approved value and ₱8.9M savings.

Impact

Procurement is creating material commercial value at scale.

Recommendation

Prioritize high-value categories and replicate strong sourcing practices.

03

Savings performance differs by category

Observation

CSA 5.7% • Electrical 12.9% • Mechanical 17.6%.

Analysis

Electrical leads absolute savings; Mechanical leads savings rate.

Impact

Scale and efficiency point to different management opportunities.

Recommendation

Benchmark categories using both savings amount and savings rate.

04

Quotation turnaround is inconsistent

Observation

Average turnaround is 9.65 days, with monthly observations near 2.8 to 20 days.

Analysis

The average masks substantial cycle-time variation.

Impact

Unpredictable quotation cycles can delay commercial decisions.

Recommendation

Set quotation SLAs and escalate RFQs approaching the threshold.

05

Aging requires exception control

Observation

Average aging is 24.16 days, with periods near 45 days; 35 of 37 RFQs are closed.

Analysis

The portfolio is largely closed, but individual cases can age materially.

Impact

The risk is concentrated in prolonged exceptions—not a large open backlog.

Recommendation

Use 7 / 14 / 30+ day aging buckets and exception-based escalation.

Executive Takeaway

Improve conversion while making RFQ execution more predictable.
