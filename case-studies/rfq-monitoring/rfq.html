1.

Overview

Project Title

RFQ Performance &
Procurement Value Analysis

Business Case

Procurement leaders need more than an RFQ count. They need to understand how sourcing activity converts into awards, how much financial value is captured, and where execution requires attention.

Business Objective

Enable leaders to identify RFQ conversion gaps, quantify sourcing value, and improve RFQ execution through outcome, savings, turnaround, aging, and category analysis.

2.

Business Questions

Business Problem

The RFQ process is generating significant procurement value, but performance is uneven across RFQ outcomes, sourcing categories, quotation turnaround, and RFQ aging. A high proportion of RFQs are not awarded, while turnaround and aging fluctuate considerably. This creates opportunities to improve conversion, sourcing effectiveness, and procurement cycle efficiency.

Key Questions

Each question links directly to a procurement decision.

  • RFQ Conversion — How effectively are RFQs converting into awarded business?
  • Procurement Value — How much approved value and savings are being generated?
  • Operational Efficiency — How efficiently are RFQs progressing through quotation?
  • Aging & Control — Where does the RFQ pipeline require management attention?
  • Category Concentration — Where are RFQ volume and financial exposure concentrated?
3.

Key Insights

What the data reveals about RFQ conversion, value and performance.

01

Conversion is the largest gap

Observation

43% of RFQs are Not Awarded: 16 of 37 RFQs.

Analysis

Not Awarded is the largest RFQ outcome category, indicating a significant gap between sourcing activity and commercial conversion.

Impact

A significant share of sourcing activity is not converting into awarded business.

Recommendation

Create a loss-reason framework by outcome, buyer, category and supplier.

02

Procurement creates measurable value

Observation

₱82.3M approved value generated ₱10.7M in procurement savings.

Analysis

The sourcing pipeline is producing meaningful financial value, with savings reaching a 13.0% rate.

Impact

Procurement is creating material commercial value at scale.

Recommendation

Prioritize high-value categories and replicate sourcing practices associated with stronger savings.

03

Category savings vary materially

Observation

Savings rates vary by category, with CSA at 5.7%, Electrical at 12.9%, and Mechanical at 17.6%.

Analysis

Mechanical demonstrates the strongest savings rate, while CSA trails the other categories.

Impact

Category-level performance points to different sourcing efficiency opportunities.

Recommendation

Benchmark categories using both savings amount and savings rate to identify scalable practices.

04

Quotation turnaround is inconsistent

Observation

Average quotation turnaround is 9.65 days, with visible month-to-month variation.

Analysis

Peaks in turnaround indicate inconsistent supplier response and quotation-cycle performance.

Impact

Slower quotation cycles can delay sourcing decisions and purchasing execution.

Recommendation

Set cycle-time targets with exception monitoring for quotations exceeding the agreed SLA.

05

RFQ aging requires exception control

Observation

Average aging is 24.16 days, with periods reaching as high as 45 days.

Analysis

The portfolio is largely closed, but individual RFQs can age materially before resolution.

Impact

Prolonged RFQs increase execution risk and reduce visibility over the active pipeline.

Recommendation

Use 7 / 14 / 30+ day aging buckets with exception-based escalation.
