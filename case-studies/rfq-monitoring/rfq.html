1.

Overview

Project Title

RFQ Monitoring Analysis

Business Case

Procurement teams need a consolidated view of RFQ activity, sourcing outcomes, approved value, savings, turnaround time, and aging to understand where commercial opportunities are being converted and where procurement performance requires attention.

Business Objective

Analyze RFQ outcomes, quotation efficiency, procurement value, savings, and aging to identify opportunities for stronger sourcing performance, faster quotation cycles, and better commercial control.

2.

Business Questions

Business Problem

Procurement stakeholders need a clearer understanding of RFQ conversion, commercial value, sourcing efficiency, and unresolved workload to identify where opportunities are being won, lost, delayed, or left without quotation.

Key Questions

The analysis was designed to answer five key questions across RFQ performance, commercial value, sourcing efficiency, and operational control.

  • How is the RFQ pipeline performing across outcomes?
  • Which RFQs are converting into awarded business?
  • How much approved value and procurement savings are being generated?
  • How efficiently are quotations being processed?
  • Where are aging and unresolved RFQs creating operational exposure?
3.

Key Insights

1.

RFQ conversion leaves a significant opportunity gap

Observation

13 of 37 RFQs were awarded, representing 35% of total RFQs, while 16 RFQs were not awarded.

Analysis

The pipeline contains more non-awarded RFQs than awarded RFQs, indicating that conversion remains an important sourcing performance opportunity.

Impact

Improving RFQ conversion could increase the commercial value captured from existing sourcing activity.

Recommendation

Review non-awarded RFQs by category, buyer, and sourcing outcome to identify recurring conversion barriers.

2.

Procurement savings demonstrate measurable commercial value

Observation

Procurement savings reached ₱10.7M, equivalent to 13.0% based on the dashboard.

Analysis

The sourcing process is generating measurable financial value while RFQ activity continues across multiple categories.

Impact

Sustaining and scaling effective sourcing practices can protect procurement value and strengthen cost efficiency.

Recommendation

Identify the sourcing practices behind the strongest savings outcomes and replicate them across comparable RFQs.

3.

Quotation turnaround varies materially across the year

Observation

Average quotation turnaround is 9.65 days, with the monthly trend showing substantial variation across the year.

Analysis

The dashboard shows a peak turnaround of approximately 20 days and a low point of approximately 2.8 days.

Impact

Longer quotation cycles can delay sourcing decisions and increase the time required to convert RFQs into commercial outcomes.

Recommendation

Investigate the causes of high-turnaround periods and establish turnaround targets for comparable RFQ types.

4.

RFQ aging requires active pipeline control

Observation

Average RFQ aging is 24.16 days, with monthly aging reaching approximately 45 days at its highest point.

Analysis

Aging is uneven across the year, indicating periods where RFQs remain active for materially longer than the overall average.

Impact

Prolonged aging can reduce pipeline visibility and delay commercial decisions on unresolved RFQs.

Recommendation

Establish aging thresholds and review long-running RFQs through a focused exception process.
