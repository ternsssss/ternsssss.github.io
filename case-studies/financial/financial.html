BUSINESS PROBLEM

Management has financial performance data across sales, products, markets, customer segments, pricing and discounts, but needs a clearer view of what is driving revenue and profit — and where commercial decisions may improve performance.

KEY QUESTIONS

03.

DATA-DRIVEN FINDINGS

Key Insights

Observation → Analysis → Impact → Recommendation

KEY INSIGHT 01

Revenue is relatively diversified across markets

01 — OBSERVATION $25.03M

USA leads revenue; Canada, France and Germany are closely grouped.

02 — ANALYSIS

What it means

The four largest markets are close in revenue contribution; Mexico is lower at $20.95M.

03 — IMPACT

Management implication

The business is not heavily dependent on one geography, reducing revenue concentration risk.

04 — RECOMMENDATION

Prioritize country × segment combinations based on demonstrated revenue contribution.

KEY INSIGHT 02

Paseo is the leading revenue and profit product

01 — OBSERVATION $33.0M

Paseo net sales, supported by 338.24K units sold.

02 — ANALYSIS

What it means

Paseo also leads product profit at $4.8M, ahead of VTT at $3.0M and Amarilla at $2.8M.

03 — IMPACT

Management implication

Paseo is a core commercial and profit pool; changes in performance can materially affect the result.

04 — RECOMMENDATION

Protect Paseo through availability, pricing discipline and targeted commercial investment.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE Matched visual from the supplied dashboard
KEY INSIGHT 03

Lower discounting has the largest profit contribution

01 — OBSERVATION $6.2M

Profit from the Low discount band; Medium contributes $5.6M and High $3.4M.

02 — ANALYSIS

What it means

The highest discount band is not the highest profit contributor.

03 — IMPACT

Management implication

Discounting can support revenue while reducing profit quality when it is not tied to incremental value.

04 — RECOMMENDATION

Use discount guardrails and evaluate promotions on incremental profit, not revenue alone.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE Matched visual from the supplied dashboard
KEY INSIGHT 04

Government is the largest revenue segment

01 — OBSERVATION $52.50M

Government net sales, ahead of Small Business at $42.43M.

02 — ANALYSIS

What it means

Government represents the largest segment revenue pool in the dashboard.

03 — IMPACT

Management implication

Large revenue pools deserve deliberate retention and commercial planning.

04 — RECOMMENDATION

Protect the Government revenue base while separately reviewing discount economics by segment.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE Matched visual from the supplied dashboard
KEY INSIGHT 05

Amarilla and VTT show the largest pricing gaps

01 — OBSERVATION $146

Amarilla: $260 selling price versus $114 manufacturing price.

02 — ANALYSIS

What it means

The absolute price gaps are $146 for Amarilla and $128 for VTT.

03 — IMPACT

Management implication

Pricing is a potential margin lever, but the dashboard does not establish an optimal price.

04 — RECOMMENDATION

Test controlled value-based pricing and monitor both volume and profit response.
