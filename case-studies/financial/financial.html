1.

Overview

Project Title

Financial Performance & Profitability Dashboard

Business Case

Management needs a consolidated view of sales, profit, product performance, customer segments, discounting, and pricing to understand where financial performance is strongest and where improvement opportunities exist.

Business Objective

Identify the key drivers of sales and profit, evaluate discount and pricing performance, and translate the findings into practical commercial actions that support profitable growth.

2.

Business Questions

Business Problem

Historical financial reporting shows what happened, but management also needs to understand where revenue is created, where profit is concentrated, and where pricing or discount decisions may be affecting profit quality.

The analysis was designed around four management decisions:

  • Sales Performance Which products and markets generate the most net sales, and how is revenue distributed across the business?
  • Profitability Which products, countries, and discount bands contribute the most profit?
  • Pricing Which products show the largest selling-price versus manufacturing-price gaps?
  • Discounts Which customer segments receive the highest discounts, and what does discount intensity mean for profit quality?
Decision Objective Turn dashboard evidence into a small number of actions that senior leaders can understand and prioritize.
3.

Key Insights

01

Revenue is geographically diversified

Observation

USA leads net sales at $25.03M, followed closely by Canada at $24.89M, France at $24.35M, and Germany at $23.51M.

Analysis

The four largest markets are relatively close in revenue contribution, while Mexico is lower at $20.95M.

Impact

The business is not heavily dependent on one geography, reducing revenue concentration risk.

Recommendation

Prioritize country × segment combinations based on demonstrated revenue contribution rather than treating every market equally.

02

Paseo is the leading revenue and profit product

Observation

Paseo generates $33.0M in net sales and approximately 338.24K units sold.

Analysis

Paseo also leads product profit at $4.8M, ahead of VTT at $3.0M and Amarilla at $2.8M.

Impact

Paseo is a core commercial and profit pool; changes in its performance can materially affect the overall result.

Recommendation

Protect Paseo through availability, pricing discipline, and targeted commercial investment.

03

Lower discounting has the largest profit contribution

Observation

The Low discount band contributes $6.2M profit, compared with $5.6M for Medium and $3.4M for High.

Analysis

The highest discount band does not produce the highest profit contribution.

Impact

Broad discounting can support revenue while weakening the quality of revenue and contribution.

Recommendation

Use discount guardrails and evaluate promotions using incremental profit rather than revenue alone.

04

Government is the largest revenue segment

Observation

Government generates $52.50M in net sales, ahead of Small Business at $42.43M.

Analysis

Government represents the largest revenue pool among the customer segments shown in the dashboard.

Impact

Large revenue pools deserve deliberate retention and commercial planning because changes can affect overall performance materially.

Recommendation

Protect the Government revenue base while separately reviewing discount economics by segment.

05

Amarilla and VTT show the largest pricing gaps

Observation

Amarilla shows a $146 gap between its $260 selling price and $114 manufacturing price.

Analysis

The absolute pricing gaps are $146 for Amarilla and $128 for VTT.

Impact

Pricing is a potential margin lever, although the dashboard does not establish an optimal price.

Recommendation

Test controlled value-based pricing and monitor both volume and profit response.
