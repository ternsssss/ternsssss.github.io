1.

Overview

Project Title

HR Analytics Dashboard — Workforce Retention & Attrition Analysis

Business Case

An organization needs a clearer view of workforce composition, hiring activity, employee tenure, and attrition patterns to identify where workforce instability is concentrated.

Business Objective

Use workforce data to identify the departments, roles, and tenure stages most exposed to attrition — then prioritize targeted retention, development, and succession actions.

2.

Business Questions

Each question links directly to a workforce decision.

•
Workforce Scale

How large is the workforce, and how is headcount distributed across departments?

•
Workforce Structure

How does the workforce differ by gender, age, salary band, and tenure?

•
Attrition Drivers

Which departments, job roles, tenure groups, and workforce segments experience the most attrition?

•
Retention Opportunity

Where should leadership focus retention, onboarding, career development, and succession efforts?

3.

Key Insights

Workforce patterns translated into management implications.

01

Retention risk is material

Observation

The dashboard reports a 99.52% attrition rate and 103 attrition cases.

Analysis

Attrition is large relative to the 310-headcount workforce and materially affects workforce stability.

Impact

Recruitment volume alone will not solve the underlying workforce issue.

Recommendation

Shift management attention toward measurable retention and attrition-reduction actions.

02

Production carries the greatest attrition exposure

Observation

Production represents 208 employees, or 67.1% of headcount, and 83 of 103 attrition cases.

Analysis

The largest workforce segment is also the dominant source of attrition.

Impact

Production has the greatest potential influence on overall workforce stability.

Recommendation

Prioritize a department-level retention program in Production.

03

Frontline production roles dominate turnover

Observation

Production Technician I accounts for 52 attrition cases; Technician II accounts for 26.

Analysis

Frontline production roles dominate Production's turnover profile.

Impact

Operational continuity and replacement effort may be concentrated in a small set of roles.

Recommendation

Target frontline career paths, supervisor support, recognition, and working conditions.

04

Early-tenure retention and succession need attention

Observation

78 leavers are in the 1–5 year group, while 22 are under one year. The workforce also skews mature, with 132 employees aged 40–49 and 102 aged 50+.

Analysis

Retention pressure exists early in tenure while the broader workforce creates future succession and knowledge-transfer needs.

Impact

The organization faces both near-term turnover and longer-term continuity risk.

Recommendation

Strengthen onboarding, career development, succession planning, and knowledge transfer.
