PROJECT OVERVIEW

ER Executive Dashboard

A consolidated Power BI dashboard designed to provide a high-level view of emergency department performance, patient demand, admissions, wait time, referrals, and patient experience.

Project Title

Hospital Emergency Department Performance & Patient Experience

Business Case

Emergency departments must balance patient demand, service capacity, waiting time, admissions, referrals, and patient experience. Without a consolidated view of these operational signals, it becomes difficult to identify pressure points and prioritize improvement.

Business Objective

Use ER visit, arrival, shift, admission, referral, wait-time, and satisfaction data to identify where operational efficiency and patient experience can be improved.

Decision Lens

The analysis focuses on three core questions:

  1. Where is patient demand concentrated?
  2. Which operating windows need attention?
  3. Where does patient experience lag?
