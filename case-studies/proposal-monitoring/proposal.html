1.

Overview

Project Title

Proposal Monitoring &
Bid Performance Analytics

Business Case

Leadership is managing a large proposal portfolio across contracts, business units and proposal owners. The challenge is not a lack of data—it is turning fragmented status, value and ownership signals into a focused management view.

Business Objective

Improve proposal governance by identifying where commercial value is concentrated, which opportunities are progressing, and where management intervention can improve conversion.

Dashboard evidence • June 13, 2026

2.

Business Questions

Business Problem

Proposal information is spread across business units, contracts, proposal owners and award stages, making it difficult to see where commercial value is concentrated, which opportunities are progressing, and where conversion risk requires management attention.

Key Questions

Each question links directly to a commercial decision.

  • Where is the proposal value? Which business units, employees and statuses hold the largest commercial exposure?
  • How healthy is the pipeline? How much value is awarded, active, under evaluation, lost, cancelled or on hold?
  • Are we converting proposals? What do awarded value and project count tell us about bid effectiveness?
  • Is activity changing? How are proposal volume and cost-estimate activity moving month to month?
  • Where should management act? Which opportunities, owners and business units need attention or stronger qualification?
3.

Key Insights

What the dashboard evidence reveals about value, pipeline, concentration, and workload.

01

Value conversion is the critical gap

Observation

₱106M was awarded from ₱5.22B of proposed value.

Analysis

Value conversion is approximately 2%, while the 73% win rate is count-based.

Impact

A strong count-based win rate can mask weak conversion of material commercial value.

Recommendation

Track win rate by proposal value and stage, not count alone.

02

Main contract dominates the commercial pipeline

Observation

The Main contract carries ₱5.10B across 208 projects, including ₱887M on-going, ₱4.02B not awarded, and ₱106M awarded.

Analysis

Main contract performance accounts for almost all portfolio proposal value, making its progression critical to the overall result.

Impact

Weak conversion in the core contract pipeline can dominate the company's commercial outcome.

Recommendation

Prioritize high-value Main bids for stage-age, probability, and next-action reviews.

03

Change Orders contain pending incremental value

Observation

Change Orders total ₱113.8M across 25 projects, with ₱83M in Client Evaluation and ₱29M On-going. No awarded amount is shown.

Analysis

Most Change Order value is still awaiting progression or a client decision.

Impact

Without active follow-up, meaningful incremental commercial value can remain unconverted.

Recommendation

Create a dedicated Change Order ageing and client-decision review to accelerate approvals.

04

CMS carries concentrated commercial exposure

Observation

CMS contributes ₱4.02B of proposal value across 38 projects.

Analysis

CMS represents approximately 77% of the ₱5.22B portfolio, with ₱3.62B not awarded versus ₱82.6M awarded.

Impact

Portfolio performance and forecast risk are highly sensitive to CMS outcomes.

Recommendation

Set CMS-specific conversion targets while building a balanced pipeline across other business units.

05

Owner workload should be managed with value

Observation

Luffy supports 17 projects—the highest project count shown—with ₱406.9M in proposal value, including ₱56M On-going and ₱108M in Client Evaluation.

Analysis

The highest workload by count does not automatically equal the highest commercial value. Luffy's pipeline is distributed across active and evaluation stages.

Impact

Workload and value should be managed together so high-volume owners do not become bottlenecks.

Recommendation

Use an owner-level review combining project count, proposal value, stage, and pending client decisions.

↗
Executive Takeaway

Prioritize high-value active bids first, then improve qualification and pipeline balance.
