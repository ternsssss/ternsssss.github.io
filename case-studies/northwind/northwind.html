1.

Overview

Project Title

Northwind Traders — Commercial Performance & Growth Analytics

Business Case

A multi-market operation needs one clear view of where revenue is created and where value is being lost across products, customers, markets, employees, and logistics.

Business Objective

Identify growth drivers, expose underperformance, and prioritize actions that improve growth, customer value, and operating efficiency.

2.

Business Questions

Business Problem

Northwind needs a clearer view of where sales are growing, what drives value, and where commercial or logistics performance can improve.

Key Questions

Five questions guide the analysis and management discussion.

  • Revenue How is sales changing?
  • Products Which products drive growth?
  • Customers Who creates the most value?
  • Markets Where can we grow?
  • Operations How can logistics improve?
Decision Objective Turn commercial evidence into focused growth, customer, market, and operating actions.
3.

Key Insights

What the dashboard evidence reveals about concentration, product mix, customer value, and operating performance.

01

Revenue concentration creates both strength and risk

Observation

USA and Germany are the two largest markets, generating $245.58K and $230.28K respectively.

Analysis

Together they contribute $475.86K, representing approximately 37.5% of the $1.27M net-sales base shown.

Impact

Core-market strength also creates geographic concentration and exposure.

Recommendation

Protect core markets while developing underpenetrated markets.

02

Product mix is concentrated

Observation

Beverages contributes 21.16% of net sales, followed by Dairy Products at 18.53%.

Analysis

The two largest categories together account for 39.69% of the net-sales base.

Impact

A concentrated product mix means a small number of categories materially influence performance.

Recommendation

Prioritize inventory, promotion, and sales attention on proven drivers.

03

High-value customers can materially influence performance

Observation

Rattlesnake Canyon Grocery is the largest premium customer shown at $10.50K.

Analysis

The March 2014 view shows 24 active customers and 30 customer orders.

Impact

High-value accounts can materially influence commercial performance.

Recommendation

Segment accounts by value and build retention and upsell actions.

04

Product performance is highly uneven

Observation

The top five growth drivers generate a combined $386.99K.

Analysis

The five listed areas of concern total only $9.63K, showing a wide performance spread.

Impact

Commercial resources should not be allocated evenly across the product portfolio.

Recommendation

Accelerate proven winners and review persistently weak products.

05

Shipping providers are a commercial lever

Observation

United Package leads sales by shipping company at $533.5K.

Analysis

Federal Shipping contributes $383.4K and Speedy Express contributes $348.8K.

Impact

Provider economics can affect cost, service, and customer experience.

Recommendation

Evaluate providers on cost, reliability, and delivery performance.

06

Core markets reveal the next growth opportunity

Observation

USA and Germany are the two largest markets in the dashboard ranking.

Analysis

The dashboard identifies the strongest markets but does not quantify an underpenetrated-market gap.

Impact

Core strength can fund targeted expansion without diluting focus.

Recommendation

Prioritize targeted market-entry strategies where demand potential is strongest.
