1.

Business Case

A single executive view connecting patient demand, care delivery, and financial performance.

PROJECT TITLE

Hospital Patient Analysis

An executive analytics portfolio for understanding patient utilization, clinical services, and healthcare cost drivers.

BUSINESS CASE → OBJECTIVE

Hospital leaders need a unified view of demand, clinical utilization, and financial burden to identify priorities and cost drivers.

Objective: turn patient, encounter, procedure, and cost data into actionable insights that support better care delivery, resource allocation, documentation, and financial decisions.

2.

Business Problem & Key Questions

Define the decision problem first, then frame the questions the dashboard must answer.

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Hospital leaders lack a single decision view connecting patient demand, clinical utilization, documentation quality, and financial burden. This makes it harder to identify cost drivers, prioritize resources, and target operational improvement.

The analysis was designed around five management decisions:

• Patient demand

Which patient segments and encounter types drive hospital utilization?

• Clinical activity

Which procedures, diagnoses, and service lines account for the greatest activity?

• Cost drivers

Which encounter classes and procedures contribute most to healthcare costs?

• Patient burden

How are healthcare costs shared between payers and patients?

• Operational gaps

Where do documentation and resource-utilization issues create improvement opportunities?

3.

Key Insights

Dashboard evidence → Observation → Analysis → Impact → Recommendation

KEY INSIGHT 1 OF 5

Financial burden is concentrated on patients

Observation

Patients carry $70.42M of the $101.5M total claim cost.

Analysis

69% of costs are out-of-pocket, while payer coverage is 31%.

Impact

Patient affordability and access may be under pressure.

Recommendation

Expand payer partnerships and patient financial-assistance pathways.

Evidence: $101.5M total claim cost • $31.10M payer coverage • $70.42M patient out-of-pocket

Dashboard Page 4 — Financial & Operational

KEY INSIGHT 2 OF 5

An aging population shapes hospital demand

Observation

645 of 974 patients are seniors, with an average age of 70.24.

Analysis

Older adults represent the dominant patient segment.

Impact

Chronic-disease and geriatric-care demand is structurally important.

Recommendation

Strengthen preventive care, follow-up, and care coordination.

Evidence: Average age 70.24 • 645 senior patients • 974 total patients

Dashboard Page 2 — Patient Demographics

KEY INSIGHT 3 OF 5

Ambulatory care is the largest activity and cost pool

Observation

Ambulatory encounters total 12.5K and generate $36.3M in costs.

Analysis

Ambulatory care leads both encounter volume and total cost.

Impact

It is a major capacity, throughput, and resource-allocation lever.

Recommendation

Expand ambulatory capacity and preventive-service pathways.

Evidence: 12.5K ambulatory encounters • $36.3M ambulatory cost

Dashboard Page 4 — Financial & Operational

KEY INSIGHT 4 OF 5

Clinical costs are concentrated in high-cost procedures

Observation

Electrical cardioversion contributes $35.8M in procedure cost.

Analysis

It accounts for more than half of the $69.2M shown across the leading procedure-cost list.

Impact

A small number of high-cost procedures can materially influence clinical spend.

Recommendation

Review high-cost pathways, resource utilization, and length of stay.

Evidence: Electrical cardioversion $35.82M • Top procedure-cost list $69.18M

Dashboard Page 3 — Clinical Services & Utilization

KEY INSIGHT 5 OF 5

Clinical documentation is a major data-quality gap

Observation

19,541 of 27,891 encounters are recorded as “No diagnosis.”

Analysis

Approximately 70% of encounters lack a recorded diagnosis in the dashboard.

Impact

This constrains clinical reporting, analytical insight, and reimbursement accuracy.

Recommendation

Strengthen physician documentation, coding accuracy, and CDI.

Evidence: “No diagnosis” 19,541 • Total clinical encounters 27,891
