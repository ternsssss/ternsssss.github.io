1.

Overview

Project Title

Food & Beverage Sales Performance Dashboard

Business Case

DSA Food and Beverage Co. is experiencing strong revenue and volume growth, but performance is concentrated across a small number of products, channels, categories, and salespeople. Management needs a consolidated commercial view to understand where growth is coming from and where margin or concentration risks may be emerging.

Business Objective

Evaluate revenue and volume growth, product and category mix, channel performance, and sales productivity to identify the strongest growth engines, concentration risks, and practical opportunities for more profitable and diversified growth.

2.

Business Questions

Business Problem

Strong top-line growth does not automatically mean stronger business quality. Management needs to determine whether growth is broad-based and profitable, or increasingly dependent on a few products, channels, categories, and salespeople.

Key Questions

The analysis was designed to answer six management questions across growth, portfolio concentration, channels, categories, sales productivity, and profitability.

  • How has revenue and sales volume changed over time?
  • Which products drive the most revenue and where is product concentration creating risk?
  • Which sales channels contribute the most revenue, and where is digital growth underleveraged?
  • Which categories are performing strongly and which require portfolio attention?
  • How concentrated is revenue across the sales team, and what does that mean for scalability?
  • How can pricing, discounting, product mix, and channel strategy support more profitable growth?
3.

Key Insights

01

Strong growth is accompanied by margin risk

Observation

Gross Revenue reached $20.37M and Total Units Sold reached 6.63M, with year-over-year growth of 59.6% in revenue and 78.6% in units.

Analysis

Unit growth is running ahead of revenue growth, indicating that volume expansion is not translating proportionally into revenue per unit.

Impact

Rapid growth can mask pricing, mix, or discount pressure if the business focuses on volume without monitoring margin quality.

Recommendation

Conduct pricing optimization, reduce unnecessary discounts, and introduce premium SKUs to improve revenue quality and margin.

02

Wheat Flour creates significant product concentration

Observation

Wheat Flour generates $5.71M in sales, representing 39.41% of total sales and 35.25% of units sold at 2.03M units.

Analysis

One product accounts for a disproportionately large share of commercial performance compared with the rest of the portfolio.

Impact

Dependence on Wheat Flour increases portfolio concentration risk and makes overall performance more sensitive to one product.

Recommendation

Diversify the portfolio through bundling, complementary products, and premium variants that can grow alongside the core product.

03

Retail dominates while Online remains underleveraged

Observation

Retail contributes $10.01M in revenue, followed by Distributor at $6.91M and Online at $3.45M.

Analysis

Retail remains the dominant channel while Online contributes a much smaller share of the current revenue base.

Impact

The channel mix creates an opportunity to build a more diversified route to market and reduce reliance on physical retail.

Recommendation

Scale digital marketing, subscriptions, and online-exclusive bundles to strengthen the Online channel.

04

Beverage performance is weak relative to Food

Observation

Food generates $18.95M, or approximately 93% of revenue, while Drink contributes only $1.42M, or 7%.

Analysis

The portfolio is heavily weighted toward Food, leaving Beverage with limited contribution to the current revenue base.

Impact

Weak beverage performance limits category diversification and leaves potential growth from higher-growth drink segments underdeveloped.

Recommendation

Reposition or rationalize weak SKUs and invest selectively in higher-growth drink segments with stronger commercial potential.

05

Revenue is concentrated among a small number of salespeople

Observation

Mikaela Ong generates $5.49M, followed by Marco Salcedo at $3.80M and Isabela Torres at $2.75M.

Analysis

The top three salespeople account for roughly three-quarters of the sales shown in the employee performance view.

Impact

Revenue concentration creates scalability and continuity risk if high-performing accounts or relationships depend heavily on a few individuals.

Recommendation

Standardize the sales playbook, rebalance accounts, and introduce cross-selling incentives to distribute revenue contribution more sustainably.

06

The next 12 months should prioritize diversified, scalable growth

Observation

The dashboard shows strong growth, but revenue is concentrated by product, channel, category, and salesperson.

Analysis

The strongest commercial opportunities are not simply about generating more volume; they are about improving mix, diversification, digital reach, and productivity.

Impact

Without diversification and margin discipline, continued growth could increase operational and commercial risk rather than strengthen business resilience.

Recommendation

Build the next 12-month growth plan around diversified channels, stronger product mix, improved beverage performance, and more scalable sales execution.
