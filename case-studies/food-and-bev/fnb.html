03.

Key Insights

What the dashboard reveals about growth quality, commercial concentration, and portfolio health.

01 GROWTH QUALITY

Strong growth — but volume is moving faster than revenue.

OBSERVATION

Revenue increased 59.6% versus prior year, while units increased 78.6%.

ANALYSIS

The faster volume growth indicates weaker revenue yield per unit or a shift toward lower-value mix.

IMPACT

If left unmanaged, headline growth can mask pressure on pricing quality and profitability.

RECOMMENDATION

Segment pricing, discounting, and product mix by channel to protect revenue yield.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE
02 PRODUCT CONCENTRATION

Wheat Flour is the dominant commercial growth engine.

OBSERVATION

Wheat Flour contributes $5.71M, equal to 39.41% of total sales.

ANALYSIS

A single product accounts for a substantial share of the portfolio's revenue base.

IMPACT

A disruption in demand, supply, or pricing could materially affect overall performance.

RECOMMENDATION

Protect the core product while expanding complementary and premium variants.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE
03 CHANNEL MIX

Retail leads; Online remains underleveraged.

OBSERVATION

Retail contributes $10.01M, Distributor $6.91M, and Online $3.45M.

ANALYSIS

Retail is the largest channel, while Online contributes the least revenue.

IMPACT

The business has an opportunity to build a more scalable and diversified route to market.

ACTION

Scale digital acquisition, online-exclusive bundles, and repeat-purchase mechanics.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE
04 CATEGORY MIX

Food dominates the portfolio.

OBSERVATION

Food contributes $18.95M (93%) versus Drinks at $1.42M (7%).

ANALYSIS

Revenue is heavily weighted toward one category.

IMPACT

Category concentration increases exposure to shifts in Food demand and limits portfolio balance.

ACTION

Reposition weak Drinks SKUs and invest in higher-growth beverage segments.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE
05 SALES EXECUTION

Revenue is concentrated among a few salespeople.

OBSERVATION

Mikaela Ong contributes 34.32% and Marco Salcedo 23.77% of revenue.

ANALYSIS

The top two salespeople account for 58.09% of dashboard revenue.

IMPACT

Commercial performance is more dependent on individual sellers than is desirable.

ACTION

Standardize high-performer practices, rebalance accounts, and strengthen cross-selling.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE
06 PRODUCT HEALTH

Weak products require active portfolio management.

OBSERVATION

Tomato Sauce is identified as an area of concern at $1.67K and 426 units.

ANALYSIS

Some SKUs contribute very little relative to the portfolio leaders.

IMPACT

Keeping weak SKUs without a recovery plan can tie up attention and commercial resources.

ACTION

Set thresholds for recovery, repositioning, or discontinuation.

DASHBOARD EVIDENCE
04.

Recommendations

Five focused actions derived directly from the dashboard evidence.

01

Protect growth quality

ACTION

Segment pricing and discounting by product and channel.

WHY IT MATTERS

Volume is growing faster than revenue, creating revenue-yield risk.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Improve revenue yield and protect profitability.

02

Diversify the portfolio

ACTION

Build bundles, complementary products, and premium variants around core winners.

WHY IT MATTERS

Wheat Flour and Food represent a large share of sales.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Reduce concentration risk and increase basket value.

03

Accelerate Online

ACTION

Invest in digital acquisition, online-exclusive bundles, and repeat-purchase mechanics.

WHY IT MATTERS

Online contributes $3.45M versus $10.01M in Retail.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Create a scalable channel with additional customer reach.

04

Strengthen sales execution

ACTION

Standardize high-performer practices and rebalance accounts.

WHY IT MATTERS

The top two salespeople contribute 58.09% of dashboard revenue.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Reduce seller dependency and spread best practices.

05

Rationalize weak SKUs

ACTION

Set clear thresholds for recovery, repositioning, or discontinuation.

WHY IT MATTERS

The dashboard identifies weak products such as Tomato Sauce.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Free resources for higher-growth products.

STRATEGIC PRIORITY Diversified, scalable growth with improved profitability and reduced operational risk.
